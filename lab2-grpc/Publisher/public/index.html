What to try
  1. Add publisher-news on news and start a Receiver on news. Send a message: it goes queued → sent → acked and the Receiver prints it.
  2. Add publisher-sports on sports: only Receivers subscribed to sports get its messages (topic isolation).
  3. Add a second publisher with a different id on news: the broker rejects the registration (one publisher per topic).
  4. Click Send 10: a Receiver started with -w 1 prints #1…#10 in order. (With several workers, the Receiver may print them out of order.)
  5. Click Drop connection, then quickly send a message: it waits as queued, and is sent once the publisher reconnects and registers again.
  6. Stop a Receiver, send a few messages, then start it again: it prints the topic's whole history (marked (history)), including what it missed, then live messages.
  7. Send a few messages on games before any Receiver listens to it, then start a new Receiver on games: the earlier messages appear right away.
  8. Pick an invalid frame and click Send invalid: the broker rejects it with a reason and logs it to its Dead Letter Queue.
